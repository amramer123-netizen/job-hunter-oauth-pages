AI Universe Job Hunter

AI Universe Job Hunter is a personal job-search assistant operated locally by its owner. It helps organize job-search information and can connect to Google Gmail with the account holder's permission.

Gmail integration

When authorized, the application requests Gmail read-only and send permissions. These permissions support email-related job-search workflows, such as finding relevant messages and preparing or sending correspondence when enabled and approved by the user. Access is controlled through Google's OAuth consent process.

The application itself is not hosted on this public website. This site provides information about the local application and its privacy practices. Visiting this site does not grant Gmail access.
